Our heritage. Plan, test, change.

A South African map, a square coding grid and twelve story stops. Choose a destination, plan the commands, move a paper token and share a story.

Download the free A2 play matMat + printing and adult notes

Print and play with paper tokens

The mat is A2 landscape, 594 × 420 mm. Print at 100% / actual size, not fit-to-page. You can use PDF poster/tile printing with overlap on a home printer; check row and column alignment before joining.

Start at (0,0), facing up. F means one square forward; R and L turn in place. Predict, test, reset position and facing direction, then change the plan. The 8 × 8 mat has different coordinates from the book's 4 × 4 practice grids.

Before using Cooper

This A2 download is sized for paper tokens, not calibrated robot movement. A Cooper-sized manufactured mat still needs adult trials to set square size, settings, surface and stopping behaviour. Printed lines are not line-following tracks.

Cooper is rated 5+. Under-fives use paper tokens or body play. Follow the product warnings and centre safety rules.

Real places. Imagined story pictures.
