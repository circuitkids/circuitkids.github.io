The Pack That Came Back

A novel by Mendi Taukwena

In 1991, six wild dogs were moved to Madikwe to bring the painted wolves back home. Told by Madikwe, the queen of the pack, this is the true-hearted story of Hlolwa and the dogs who walked through fire so the pack could live. "Brave is being scared, and doing the right thing anyway."

179 pages · English · Best for readers 14 and up · 5.0 MB

The Adventures of Madikwe: The Butterfly Chase

Story 1 - by Mendi Taukwena

Madikwe is the littlest pup in the pack, and the loudest. One morning she chases a yellow butterfly far into the tall grass - and loses her way. Then she remembers what Aunt Laura says: stop, stay where you are, and listen. A story about bravery, family, and finding your way back to the pack.
