The Pack That Came Back

The wild dogs of Madikwe, brought home. A novel by Mendi Taukwena.

"Brave is being scared, and doing the right thing anyway."

In 1991, six wild dogs were moved to Madikwe to bring the painted wolves back home. Told by Madikwe, the queen of the pack, this is the story of Hlolwa and the dogs who walked through fire so the pack could live.

The true story

This story is a work of fiction, but the history is real. Operation Phoenix was one of the largest wildlife translocations in history, and it brought wild dogs back to Madikwe. The True Story, at the back of the book, tells it plainly.

Read it your way

Chapter by chapter at bedtime, or all in one sitting under a tree. No quiz, no homework - just the pack. Download it once and it works with no internet at all.

Imagined characters, real heart

The dogs and people in this novel are imagined, but their names honour real reserves and the rangers, vets and researchers who held the line. Madikwe is real, and it is waiting for you.
