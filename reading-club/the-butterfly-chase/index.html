The Butterfly Chase

The Adventures of Madikwe, Story 1. A story for little readers by Mendi Taukwena.

"Brave is being scared, and doing the right thing anyway."

Madikwe is the littlest pup in the pack - and the loudest. When she chases a yellow butterfly into the tall grass and loses her way, she must remember what Aunt Laura says: stop, stay where you are, and listen.
