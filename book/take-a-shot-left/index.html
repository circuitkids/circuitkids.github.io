Read together, your way

At bedtime, just share the story. No robot, quiz or homework is needed. The chapter activities are invitations for another day, with speaking, drawing, pointing and quiet observation all welcome.

Paper first

The book includes 4 × 4 paper practice grids. The companion map mat uses an 8 × 8 grid with different coordinates. F means one square forward; L and R turn in place.

Cooper is rated 5+. Under-fives use paper tokens or body play. Follow the product warnings and centre safety rules. The A2 mat is not calibrated for robot movement.

Real places. Imagined stories.

Illustrations and characters are imagined story scenes, not site photographs or historical reconstructions. Separate heritage anchors and sources appear inside the book.
