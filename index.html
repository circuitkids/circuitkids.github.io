Circuit Kids · Coding and robotics for Grade R to 7

Circuit Kids is a non-profit coding and robotics programme for South African learners, built to work in real classrooms with or without internet. Registration in progress.

Illustration.

Who we are

Circuit Kids is a legacy project for underprivileged South African children. A child's language or background should never be a barrier to digital skills. So the core programme stays the same everywhere, and the story layer changes to fit each school and community.

Quality coding education that works on slow data, in any language, in any classroom.

The programme

A STEAM pathway from Grade R to Grade 7. Art and design sit next to science, technology, engineering and maths, not behind them.

1
Grades R to 3: Cooper robotScreen-free. Little ones learn sequencing and logic by guiding a robot with their hands.
2
Grades 4 to 7: micro:bitStart with MakeCode blocks, move on to MakeCode Arcade for games, then step up to Python.

Our curriculum is mapped to the DBE Coding and Robotics CAPS competencies for Grades R to 7.

How we teach

  • Offline-first: lessons run without internet, so no school is left out.
  • Hands-on: every session is Connect, Learn, Build, Test, Share.
  • Light on cost: learner-owned kits and low monthly fees.
  • Local stories: books and examples that reflect the places children live in.

The Township edition book

A beginner micro:bit book with 20 projects, set in the streets and stories of the township. The same projects are being re-told for rural and Christian communities. The Township edition is a pilot and workshop edition.

Schools, parents and partners

We work with schools that want to offer coding and robotics, with parents who want it for their children, and with funders and partners who want to help more learners reach it. Launching January 2027.
